Methodology & definitions+

Monthly cash-flow model

Income, expenses, interest, debt payments, and liquid savings update each month across the chosen horizon.

Stochastic simulation

Each future samples economic assumptions and can introduce a disruption, producing a distribution rather than a single forecast.

Historical-style scenarios

Named scenarios are simplified stress environments inspired by historical conditions; they do not recreate every detail of the actual events.

Failure definition

A simulated run is classified as a failure if liquid savings are exhausted or the modeled debt is not cleared within the selected horizon.

Resilience score

The 0–100 score is an internal model indicator combining liquidity, debt-payment room, and shock tolerance. It is not a standardized financial rating.

Educational use

Results depend on the assumptions you enter. Use the tool to explore relationships and tradeoffs, not to make a financial decision by itself.
