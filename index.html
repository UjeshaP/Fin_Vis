How the model works

Monthly cash-flow model

Income, expenses, interest, debt payments, and liquid savings update each month.

Stochastic simulation

Each future samples economic conditions and can introduce shocks, producing a distribution rather than one forecast.

Historical scenarios

Named scenarios are “style” approximations, not claims that the model recreates every detail of a historical event.

Failure definition

A run fails if liquid savings are exhausted or modeled debt is not cleared within the horizon.
